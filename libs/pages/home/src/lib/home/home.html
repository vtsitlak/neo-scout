NEO Scout helps you follow newly discovered near-Earth objects (NEOs) — small Solar System bodies whose orbits bring them close to Earth — using live candidate data from NASA/JPL Scout.

The Scout board surfaces identity, NEO and PHA interest scores, close approach distance, MOID, magnitudes, observation count, and arc length so you can see how well an object is constrained and how urgent it looks for follow-up. That early picture matters: short arcs and uncertain orbits are when new observations reduce risk most.

Open Scout to search and filter the live list, or Contact us if you have questions about the data or the app.
